01 · Today

Know where to go.

The next stop leads with arrival time, services, access instructions, directions, and contact. The remaining route sits below; a map is one tap away.

02 · Current visit

Know the property.

Access and pet instructions, the customer’s request, previous findings, and today’s service scope come together before starting work.

03 · Record service

Confirm what happened.

Review a planned batch with multiple products. Confirm actual amounts, select treated areas, record observations, and attach photos to the same visit.

04 · Review & finish

See every outcome.

Review the recorded work once, then see separate states for the service record, pending photos, customer report, and billing.

What to try

  1. Open the sample stop from Today and start service.
  2. Confirm the actual amount for each training product. Select the areas you treated, record a finding, and add a sample photo.
  3. Visit Tools and return to the active service. Use More to explore the offline simulation.
  4. Review the service and choose Finish visit to confirm the record. Notice that saving the visit does not mark a report sent.

The design direction

Waves blue and navy, white surfaces, readable text, large primary actions, and a single bottom navigation: Today · Messages · Tools · More. Important property context stays close to the work. Manual documentation remains available without AI.

About this preview

This is an interactive design concept with fictional properties and illustrative training products. Photos, uploads, offline behavior, and the proposed Messages inbox are simulated. Actions remain in the viewer’s browser. The preview does not contact customers, change appointments, or create charges.
