DESIGN PRINCIPLES
Today, Messages, Tools, More bottom navigation. Waves blue and navy with white surfaces. Readable controls and approximately 50–52 px primary action heights. Actual treatment requires technician confirmation. Multiple protocol-approved products can share a batch. Manual documentation does not depend on AI. Service save, upload, report delivery, and billing are distinct outcomes. Internal notes and access details stay out of customer reports.

CONCEPT SCOPE
This is a design concept using fictional properties and illustrative training products. Actual photo capture, uploads, offline synchronization, and the proposed Messages inbox are simulated. Sample records stay in the viewer’s browser. No customer communications, appointment changes, or charges occur. Existing Waves permissions, approved protocols, billing rules, and specialized certificate requirements remain implementation constraints.
